01 The gap

Wallets were built for humans. Agents inherited the keys.

A wallet knows balance.
It does not know intent.

Give an agent a key and you have given it the ability to spend. The model decides what is reasonable; the wallet has no opinion. That gap is where recursive loops, wrong payees, and stale delegations become losses.

The fix is not a better prompt. It is a smaller blast radius.

A / 01

Runaway loop

The agent retries until the balance is gone. Spend scales with model error.

Contained by throttle → daily limit → per-tx limit
A / 02

Wrong payee

A hijacked prompt routes USDC to an address nobody approved.

Contained by allowlist + payee binding
A / 03

Stale delegation

After re-authorization, old signatures still look cryptographically valid.

Contained by INV-6 / epoch bump
02 Control plane

Deterministic checks. No model in the trust path.

The agent proposes.
The vault disposes.

Three checks stand between intent and settlement. Each one is deterministic; none depend on the model behaving.

01dryRun()

Simulate

Resolve allowlist, counterparty score, tier band, and rolling allowance before a signature exists. Ambiguity halts.

no capital moves
02signVoucher()

Sign

A cumulative EIP-712 voucher binds agent, merchant, token, amount, expiry, and epoch. The prompt leaves only as a hash.

replay-resistant
03claimVoucher()

Settle

The vault re-checks the delta on-chain: monotonic, bounded, inside the rolling 24h ceiling, under the active epoch.

01

Policy is bytecode, not copy

Deltas are settled against the vault's own state. The client cannot raise its own ceiling, skip a check, or talk the merchant into a larger claim.

02

Intent stays local

keccak256(prompt || timestamp) travels in the voucher memo. The raw prompt never crosses the trust boundary.

03

Pay only for delivered streams

Metered streams hold only the ceiling. The client signs exact consumption at completion and signs nothing when the stream fails.

Six properties the vault cannot be argued out of.

ID Invariant Enforcement
INV-1 Monotonic cumulative claim per (agent, merchant, epoch). InvalidVoucherAmount()
INV-2 Rolling 24h ceiling across 25 hourly slots. No oracle, no cron. DailyLimitExceeded()
INV-3 Per-transaction delta never exceeds maxPerTx. MaxPerTxExceeded()
INV-4 Voucher and policy TTL are bounded by block time. SessionExpired()
INV-5 Only the owner exits capital; no pooled liquidity, yield, or delegatecall. emergencyWithdraw / owner-only
INV-6 Vouchers bind to the active delegation epoch. Re-authorization orphans stale signatures. SessionEpochMismatch()

04 Settlement

One control plane. Two rails. Never a competing bridge.

We don't move the rail.
We decide who may use it.

Paythos merchants settle through the vault. Circle marketplace services settle through Circle. The same policy gate runs before either signature; the marketplace batching path is still a probe, not a shipped rail.
